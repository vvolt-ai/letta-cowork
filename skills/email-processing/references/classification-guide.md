# Email classification

Assign one primary category from the skill's vocabulary. Choose by the main unresolved requested action after reading the body, relevant history and attachments. Retain secondary issues explicitly; do not use rigid precedence to hide complaints, financial risk or security concerns.

| Category | Use when |
| :--- | :--- |
| Placing a Purchase Order | A customer submits or confirms an actual order; inspect the content rather than inferring from a filename |
| Requesting a Quote | Pricing, availability or lead time is requested without an order commitment |
| New Business Inquiry / Inbound Lead | A prospect explores a business relationship or capability |
| Technical Support (pre/post sales) | Product behavior, specifications, troubleshooting or compatibility is the main request |
| RMA Request | Return, replacement or warranty action is explicitly requested |
| Order Status Inquiry | Status or delivery information for an existing order is requested |
| Documentation & Compliance Request | A certificate, calibration report, declaration or other document is requested |
| Invoice & Payment Inquiry | Billing, payment, credits or refunds are the main request |
| Complaint / Escalation | Resolving escalated dissatisfaction is the main action, not merely an irritated tone |
| Other | No listed category fits; explain the actual request rather than treating all Other mail as noise |

Do not infer an RMA from the word defective if the sender only requests troubleshooting. Do not infer a new order from an old PO attached to a support thread. A requested accredited calibration report is not satisfied by relabeling a CoC. Classify internal/legal/personal/administrative mail as Other where needed, but preserve consequential actions and sensitivity.

## Confidence and mixed cases

High means the requested action is clear. Medium means one material interpretation is uncertain. Low means intent or essential content is missing. Explain uncertainty only when it affects the next action. Low confidence is not a reason to discard an urgent risk; ask the smallest question that resolves ambiguity.

Classification confidence does not establish technical correctness, sender authenticity or authority. Keep attention/status decisions in [attention and status](attention-and-status.md).

Examples:

1. A PO with a payment mismatch: primary Placing a Purchase Order, secondary payment discrepancy, proposed commercial-review reason.
2. A complaint about an unpaid partial refund: primary Complaint / Escalation if escalation resolution is the main request, secondary financial recovery, financial-exception reason; preserve the amount variance.
3. A calibration report request containing an alternative product suggestion: primary Documentation & Compliance Request, secondary replacement evaluation; do not approve equivalence.
4. A question about grounding with an old quotation attached: primary Technical Support (pre/post sales), not Placing a Purchase Order.
5. A routine quotation copied to the CEO: primary Requesting a Quote; verify execution ownership before assigning attention to the CEO.
6. A bank change in a vendor invoice: primary Invoice & Payment Inquiry with security-review reason; do not update payment data from the email alone.
