# Final review checklist

Use this checklist before completing Standard or Deep Parkinglot work.

## Scope and source

1. Confirm the exact artifact and version.
2. Confirm the correct repository location.
3. Confirm the intended reader and objective.
4. Confirm sensitivity and audience.
5. Confirm original source material was retrieved.
6. Confirm approved unaffected content was preserved.
7. Confirm similarly named artifacts were disambiguated before editing.

## Front door and navigation

1. Confirm the front README is concise enough to orient a new reader.
2. Confirm navigation is organized by reader question or decision.
3. Confirm deeper detail is linked rather than buried.
4. Confirm every new folder has a useful navigation purpose.
5. Confirm the nearest parent README was updated when structure changed.
6. Confirm links resolve.

## Claims and evidence

1. Classify consequential claims.
2. Link material facts and numbers to named sources.
3. Preserve contradictory evidence.
4. Label assumptions and illustrative examples.
5. Confirm presentation quality did not increase certainty.
6. Confirm no narrow authorization or capability became broader in prose.
7. Confirm freshness and source independence where consequences are material.

## Owners and actionability

1. Confirm decision owner.
2. Confirm operational owner.
3. Confirm required contributors and reviewers.
4. Confirm external dependencies.
5. Confirm next action and blocker.
6. Confirm the document does not assign authority it does not possess.

## Audience and language

1. Remove internal status or engineering diary language from external and user content.
2. Define unfamiliar terms.
3. Confirm examples are useful, anonymized, and labeled when illustrative.
4. Confirm confidential details are not exposed unnecessarily.
5. Confirm translated content preserves machine interpreted tokens, including usernames, placeholders, variables, paths, filenames, hostnames, service names, commands, API fields, and literal output. Use `authoring-electronic-equipment-manuals` for the full rule.
6. Confirm each audience sees only relevant detail.

## STORM and critique

1. Record the selected STORM mode and reason.
2. Confirm initial perspective reviews were independent.
3. Confirm thesis, antithesis, and synthesis for Standard or Deep work.
4. Confirm claim, contradiction, assumption, and missing evidence ledgers.
5. Confirm material findings use the required finding contract.
6. Confirm blocking findings were repaired and retested.
7. Confirm coverage and skipped dimension debt are explicit.
8. Confirm residual risk is visible.

## Diagrams and rendered artifacts

1. Confirm every diagram answers a specific question.
2. Render and inspect every changed diagram.
3. Build every required output.
4. Open the actual output.
5. Inspect every changed page or slide.
6. Check wrapping, overflow, spacing, contrast, hierarchy, tables, images, warnings, and page breaks.
7. Inspect every required language and audience variant.
8. Do not equate a successful build with publication readiness.
9. Confirm Mermaid navigation uses the GitHub URL format required by the live standard rather than relative Markdown click targets.

## Delivery status

1. Confirm no undisclosed high severity finding remains.
2. Confirm unresolved product facts are stated as blockers.
3. Confirm final, approved, released, compliant, or ready language is authorized and evidenced.
4. Confirm temporary STORM artifacts and raw review material were not committed without durable value.
5. Report path, changes, review mode, validation, repairs, residual risk, and revision identifier.
6. Confirm a dated change log entry exists for a material update.
7. Confirm engineering context exists for engineering, hardware, firmware, platform, or validation content.
