# Content type routing

Classify content before choosing its structure and review method.

## Research note

Use for grounded exploration without a committed decision.

Require sources, claims, contradictions, confidence, open questions, and next research action. Use Light STORM for narrow work and Standard STORM for substantial work.

## Project note

Use for active work with owners, workstreams, decisions, blockers, and a graduation target.

Use a project front door when more than one reader question or workstream exists. Standard STORM is normally appropriate for initial structure and major revisions.

## Decision candidate

Use for a choice requiring authority.

Require alternatives, decision criteria, evidence for and against, assumptions, consequences, owner, and explicit decision status. Use Standard or Deep STORM according to consequence.

## Engineering issue

Use for a reproducible technical problem.

Require system boundary, affected revision, reproduction, expected and observed behavior, evidence, failure impact, owner, containment, proposed repair, and verification. Add safety, manufacturing, service, and lifecycle reviewers when relevant.

Add the engineering context section required by the live standard, including product or subsystem, affected revision, requirement versus preference, affected interfaces, related decisions, and risk if ignored.

## Process draft

Use for a proposed repeatable workflow.

Require trigger, owner, inputs, steps, decision points, exceptions, evidence produced, handoffs, completion condition, and escalation boundaries. Include an operator and failure path reviewer.

## Customer or supplier input

Use for durable external feedback that may affect product, operations, or decisions.

Protect confidential details. Separate direct statements from interpretation. Preserve source and date. Do not convert a request into a commitment.

## Validation result

Use for test or experiment outcomes.

Require method, environment, sample, expected result, observed result, limitations, reproducibility, and consequence. Do not generalize a laboratory observation into a product rating.

## Manual or operational guide

Invoke `authoring-electronic-equipment-manuals` for electronic equipment manuals, quick start guides, installation, commissioning, operation, troubleshooting, maintenance, safety, connectors, pinouts, or bilingual technical instructions.

Use Deep STORM when safety, equipment damage, credentials, production, service, or customer use is involved.

## Presentation or rendered report

Use the appropriate artifact creation workflow. Classify every material claim before polishing. Render and inspect every changed page or slide. Build success is not visual or publication approval.

## Graduation pointer

Use after content moves to its canonical destination.

Record the destination, graduation date, current canonical source, preserved historical value, and any residual open issue. Do not duplicate the new canonical content.

Select the destination using the live standard. Typical destinations are ViKi, an engineering repository, a decision log, Odoo, agent memory, or closure in place. Graduation does not authorize an agent to write to a destination where it lacks authority.

## Do not create

Do not create Parkinglot entries for routine daily status, raw transcripts, unshaped agent output, duplicate ViKi content, credentials, secrets, Level 4 restricted content, or material with no durable future use.
