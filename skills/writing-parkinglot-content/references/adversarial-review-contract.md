# Adversarial review contract

Use this reference to integrate `researching-with-storm` into Parkinglot authoring. The STORM skill remains canonical for its full protocol, critique taxonomy, evidence ledgers, BITCH audit mode, repair process, and validators.

## Prewriting attack

Before polished prose for Standard and Deep work:

1. Define the reader outcome and failure stakes.
2. State the emerging thesis.
3. Define falsification criteria.
4. Identify dominant assumptions and unavailable evidence.
5. Run independent perspectives.
6. Build supporting, refuting, contradiction, assumption, and missing evidence ledgers.
7. Produce the strongest antithesis.
8. Create a synthesis that preserves supported conflict.
9. Build the outline from the synthesis.

## Minimum independent roles

Use these five roles for Standard work:

1. Outsider or novice
2. Hostile skeptic
3. Domain expert
4. Operator or maintainer
5. Evidence auditor

Add roles for safety, security, privacy, compliance, accessibility, localization, manufacturing, service, cost, simplicity, excluded stakeholders, misuse, or lifecycle when applicable.

Initial reviews must be independent. Self review is weaker evidence and must be labeled as such.

For Light mode only, correlated self review is acceptable when parallel reviewers are unavailable and the limitation is disclosed. It cannot satisfy the independence gate for Standard or Deep work.

## Claim ledger minimum

For each consequential claim, record:

1. Claim text
2. Claim class
3. Supporting evidence
4. Refuting or conflicting evidence
5. Confidence
6. Evidence freshness and independence
7. Consequence if wrong
8. Required document treatment

Claim classes are confirmed, observed, inferred, hypothetical, illustrative, open, conflicting, proposed, and approved.

Claim classes describe individual statements. They do not replace the entry level `Confidence` values defined by the live Parkinglot standard.

## Artifact attack

After drafting, test at least:

1. Intent and reader fit
2. Cold start comprehension
3. Domain correctness
4. Evidence and source independence
5. Scope and authority
6. Ownership and operational completeness
7. Failure, boundary, misuse, cleanup, and recovery
8. Safety, security, privacy, and compliance
9. Maintenance, service, manufacturing, and lifecycle
10. Structure, navigation, language, accessibility, and localization
11. Simplicity, deletion, cost, dependency, and lock in
12. Visual and rendered quality
13. Review integrity and correlated reviewer risk

## Finding contract

Every material finding must include:

1. Severity
2. Confidence
3. Exact artifact evidence
4. Affected reader or stakeholder
5. Trigger condition
6. Failure path
7. Impact and blast radius
8. Root cause
9. Smallest effective repair
10. Verification method

Do not lead with praise. Do not weaken a supported defect for tone.

## Repair contract

For every blocking finding:

1. Establish a before state.
2. Apply the smallest effective repair.
3. Run targeted verification.
4. Run negative, boundary, regression, and operational checks when applicable.
5. Record after state evidence.
6. Reinvoke the relevant independent reviewer.
7. Preserve residual risk.

## Coverage accounting

Mark every applicable dimension as covered, partial, gap, blocked, or not applicable with reason.

Do not call a review comprehensive when an applicable dimension is missing, a major conclusion lacks independent evidence, executable testing was skipped, a high severity issue remains unresolved, or the artifact version is uncertain.

## Parkinglot preservation rule

Do not commit full STORM scaffolds by default. Preserve only durable items needed by future readers:

1. Material source and claim evidence
2. Important contradictions
3. Decision relevant assumptions
4. Unresolved blockers
5. Findings that explain the final conclusion or structure
6. Verification evidence needed to reproduce a result

Keep reviewer transcripts, redundant source notes, temporary prompts, and generated scaffolds outside Parkinglot unless they have independent evidentiary value.
